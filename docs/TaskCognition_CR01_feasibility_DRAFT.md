## Problem and rationale

The current Section 5 requires at least 99% strict-valid-FINAL completion in each of twelve mode-by-family cells, first at 1,024 and then at 2,048. P02A/P02B show repeated formatting and truncation problems, but their six-input diagnostics do not constitute the formal cap test.

The paper's main question concerns learning the expected score benefit and independent-draw spoilage of choosing R over D under cost and harm constraints. Its scoring contract already assigns zero to admitted failures without a valid retainable answer. From those definitions, the targets remain well-defined when completion is below 99%. The completion threshold is a chosen experimental eligibility condition; it is not itself the bounded-score assumption used by the audit. This does not establish that an affordable, powerful or audit-passing revised study exists.

This draft proposes changing that eligibility condition openly, before final labels, while keeping the central gate comparison and its statistical standards intact.

## Consequences and limits

The question becomes explicitly about routing between these **fixed-budget answer packages**, including their format compliance and truncation risk. A learned benefit can arise from better task solving, better required-format compliance, fewer truncated answers, or a combination. Do not call the resulting difference a pure improvement in reasoning ability.

Frequent direct failures can reduce the direct-perfect denominator relevant to the spoilage constraint. A low estimated harm numerator is not evidence of safety if that denominator is inadequate; the existing audit must still reject the candidate when its denominator check fails. Costs, power, target noise and comparative advantage may still make the study infeasible or its claim unsupported.

Keep native-payload-only inspection as a clearly labelled development diagnostic. It does not replace primary scores or enter gate labels. Any proposed confirmatory secondary analysis of failure mechanisms must be specified before TEST and must not alter the primary endpoint.

The choice of 2,048 and this amendment are informed by P02A/P02B. Disclose that development history, preserve all unsuccessful attempts, and freeze the revised design before fresh final labels. Fresh data support prospective evaluation of the revised design; they do not make the design choice independent of its development evidence.
