| Proposed measurement | What it could establish | What remains missing |
|---|---|---|
| Existing 24-answer/26-forward fixed-action service-boundary diagnostic | Timings and completeness of that particular feature-before-answer lifecycle; partition/residual and cancellation observations | Finite physical support; learned head/calibration cost; directly observed gate-bypassed references; numerical equivalence across lifecycles |

The first row is a **service-boundary diagnostic**, not reference calibration. It may help instrument a policy but cannot alone freeze c_D_ref, c_R_ref or beta. Different code paths can affect caches, initialization and scheduling; subtraction of a component is not evidence from the missing counterfactual path. Neither the present worker means nor the warm 0.05368644-second feature-only measurement is a complete gate or serving reference.

An eventual reference design needs directly exercised bypass-D and bypass-R under the same declared policy used for learned-method deployment: startup allocation, bounded eligible input population, outcome-independent balanced selection, cache/thermal policy, failure/admission rules and all mandatory response/cleanup charges. Prespecify the reference aggregation (proposed arithmetic mean with equal family weights, including valid retained failures, with uncertainty reported) and its freeze timing before tuning. Invalid/unresolved accounting blocks the estimate rather than being omitted. Keep beta=c_D_ref+0.5*(c_R_ref-c_D_ref); do not inflate it for expensive features or individual learned methods. If c_R_ref<=c_D_ref, report the fact, not an invented gap.
